Happy birthday, Lil Lady ♡

Happy birthday to the girl who makes my world feel softer, brighter, and infinitely more beautiful just by being in it. If I could give you anything today, it would be the chance to see yourself through my eyes, so you could understand how wonderfully rare you are, and how much happiness your existence brings me. There are so many ways I could start this, but maybe the simplest truth is, you make everything softer. Even on my heaviest days, the thought of you feels like a quiet place my always returns to. You are the gentleness in my chaos, the calm in my noise, and the I didn't know I needed until you existed in my life. I love how you see the world, the way you notice small things, the way you feel so deeply, the way you turn ordinary moments into something . Distance can keep us apart in places, but it never keeps us apart in feeling. Because no matter where we are, my thoughts find you. You are my favourite what-if that turned into a reality and you are the reason my world feels a little more like home. You are the genesis of my soul, and you shall be its final silence; the first whisper of my existence and the last echo that fades when eternity closes its eyes. I Love you.. I Love you till Eternity and Beyond.
